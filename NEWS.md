# IsletCalc 0.0.1

* Initial release.
* `insulin_release()`: beta-cell insulin release indices (HOMA-beta, CIR,
  Stumvoll, Xinsdg30, Xinsg30, Di, Bigtt_air, Dibig) following
  Madsen et al. (2024) <doi:10.1038/s42255-024-01140-6>.
* `glucagon_release()`: fasting glucagon and glucagon AUC.
* `glucagon_resistance()`: glucagon suppression ratio and fasting
  glucagon:insulin ratio.
